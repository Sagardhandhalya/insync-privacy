Delete your account and data

Effective date: 22 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page explains how to unpair from a partner in InSync and how to request deletion of your account and the data we store on our servers.

App: InSync (package com.sagar0.insync)
Developer: Sagar Dhandhalya
Contact: dhandhalyasagar0@gmail.com

1. Unpair in the app (does not delete your account)

If you only want to disconnect from your current partner:

  1. Open InSync.
  2. Long-press your partner and confirm Unpair.

Unpairing disconnects the pair and removes both canvas links from our servers. You can pair with someone again afterwards. Your anonymous account, display name, and device token remain until you ask us to delete the account.

2. Request account and data deletion by email

The current version of InSync does not include an in-app “Delete account” button. To delete your account and the associated server data, send an email to:

dhandhalyasagar0@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The subject line “InSync account deletion request”
  • The display name you use in InSync
  • Any extra detail that helps us find your anonymous account, such as an invite code you created or used, an approximate install or pairing date, or a screenshot of the app showing your name

We will verify the request as reasonably as we can and complete deletion within 30 days, or sooner if a shorter period is required by law. If we need more information to locate your account, we will reply to your email.

3. What we delete

When we fulfil a deletion request, we delete from our servers:

  • Your anonymous authentication account and profile, including your display name
  • Device tokens used for push delivery
  • Invite codes you created
  • Pairing records you belong to (this also unpairs your partner)
  • Doodle images you uploaded and the related canvas records

4. What may be kept or remain elsewhere

  • On your partner’s device: doodles already downloaded to their phone or widget cache may remain until they clear or uninstall the app. We cannot erase copies that already live on another device.
  • On your device: uninstalling InSync, or clearing the app’s data in Android settings, removes local drafts, cached images, and preferences. Uninstalling does not by itself delete your server account; please email us for that.
  • Limited legal or security records: we may retain a minimal record of the deletion request, or other information we are required to keep temporarily for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

5. Privacy Policy

For a full description of the information InSync collects and how it is used, see our Privacy Policy.
