Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sagar Dhandhalya (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the InSync Android application (package name com.sagar0.insync).

InSync is a private 1-to-1 doodle exchange app for couples and close friends. You choose a display name, pair with one partner, and send drawings that appear on each other’s home-screen widget.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at dhandhalyasagar0@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run InSync.

Account identifiers

When you first use the app, we create an anonymous account through Supabase Auth. This account is identified by a random user ID. The current Android app does not ask for an email address, password, or phone number, and it does not collect a profile photo.

Our backend profile record can technically store an email address or avatar URL, but the shipping Android app always leaves those fields empty.

Display name

During onboarding you enter a display name. We store it on your device and in your profile on our servers. Your paired partner can see this name.

User-generated content and pairing data

We store the doodles you draw and send (image files in private cloud storage), plus the data needed to pair and sync: invite codes, pair membership, canvas records, and related timestamps.

Device identifiers used for push delivery

To update your partner’s home-screen widget when the app is closed, we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token and record the device platform (for example, “android”). These tokens are used only to send silent data messages that refresh the widget. InSync does not show marketing notifications and does not include an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps local data such as your display name and onboarding state, in-progress drawing drafts, cached doodle images, and widget data. Some of this local data may be included in Android backup, because app backup is enabled.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise or approximate location, your contacts, your camera roll, payment or financial information, health data, or an advertising ID for advertising or tracking. Doodles are created inside the app; we do not access your photo library.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account
  • Let you pair with one partner and exchange doodles
  • Show the latest received doodle on the home-screen widget
  • Deliver silent push messages so the widget can refresh when the app is closed
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service

We do not use your information to show ads, build advertising profiles, or sell data.

3. Third-party services

InSync uses the following service providers to operate the app. Each processes data according to its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — anonymous authentication, database, private file storage, realtime sync, and an edge function that triggers partner push delivery. Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — silent data push to Android devices. Privacy policies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any advertising SDK.

4. How information is shared

  • With your partner: your display name and the doodles you send are visible to the person you are currently paired with.
  • With service providers: Supabase and Google process the data described above only to provide the service.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-app advertising or advertising purposes.

5. Data retention

We keep your account, profile, pairing records, device tokens, and uploaded doodles for as long as your account exists and you use InSync.

Unpairing disconnects you from your partner and removes the linked canvas records. It does not delete your account. Local drafts and cached images stay on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall InSync.

6. Account and data deletion

The app currently has no in-app “Delete account” button. You can unpair from a partner inside the app, which disconnects the pair and removes canvas links, but leaves your account in place.

To delete your InSync account and the associated server data, follow the steps on our Account and data deletion page, or email dhandhalyasagar0@gmail.com with a deletion request. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

7. Children’s privacy

InSync is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and a private storage bucket with access limited by account and pairing rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of InSync after an update means you accept the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
